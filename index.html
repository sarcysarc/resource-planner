Candy Planner

Compare two Pokémon from the same evolution line and see which one deserves your Stardust and Candy, for each kind of battle.

1 Add screenshots

In the game, take these for each of the two Pokémon you are choosing between:

  • Its screen, the one with the Power Up button. Scroll down and take another to show its moves and Candy.
  • Its appraisal: tap the menu button on that screen, then Appraise.
  • If you like, your item bag (for Rare Candy) and your profile (for your trainer level).

2 Check the details

Find them in the game

Paste one into the Pokémon search, then screenshot the ones you might use. Regional forms share a search; the game cannot tell them apart.

    Your resources (leave blank for unlimited)

    From your bag and profile. Blank Rare Candy means none; a blank trainer level means no limit.

    3 See the answer

    How it decides: for each kind of battle, both Pokémon are powered up as far as that battle allows, and the one that ends up stronger wins. If they end up within 1% of each other, the cheaper one wins. Costs count only what is still to spend.
