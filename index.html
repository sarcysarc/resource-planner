Candy Planner

Compare Pokémon from the same evolution line and see which one deserves your Stardust and Candy, for each kind of battle.

1 Add screenshots

In the game, take one screenshot of each Pokémon you are choosing between:

  • Its appraisal: open the Pokémon, tap the menu button, then Appraise.
  • If you like, your item bag (for Rare Candy) and your profile (for your trainer level).

The planner says if another screenshot of a Pokémon would help. Add more at any time.

2 Check the details

Find them in the game

Paste one into the Pokémon search, then screenshot the ones you might use. Regional forms share a search; the game cannot tell them apart.
